# DONECASH — Brand and Design Source Inventory

## Included original assets

- `Assets/Done Cash.ai` — supplied Illustrator master, preserved byte-for-byte. Its internal layers/license have not been independently reviewed.
- Eight PNG and eight SVG exports: three app-icon backgrounds, three primary-logo backgrounds, Black Label and White Label.
- Background variants: Paper Cream, Static Black and Sticker Khaki.

Use SVG for scalable web/vector use and suitable PNG exports for raster previews. Do not treat these exports as automatically complete App Store/adaptive-icon submissions; verify platform dimensions, layers, transparency and naming at release time.
